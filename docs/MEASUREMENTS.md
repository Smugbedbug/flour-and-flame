# Measurement rules

All conversions are **display only**. The saved recipe keeps its original numbers.

## Butter (US standard)

- 1 stick = ½ cup = 8 tablespoons = 4 oz = **113.4 g**
- 1 tablespoon = **14.175 g**

Butter lines show grams, tablespoons (nearest ½) and sticks (nearest ¼), all derived from the same gram value so they scale together:
170 g ≈ 12 tbsp ≈ 1½ sticks; at 2×: 340 g ≈ 24 tbsp ≈ 3 sticks.
Applies to ingredients named "butter" (salted/unsalted). It does not apply to peanut, nut or cocoa butter, buttermilk, or already-browned butter.

## Spoons, cups and counts

Shown in quarter increments (¼, ½, ¾). If rounding changes the value by more than about 4%, the amount is prefixed with ≈.
Amounts under ⅛ tsp show as "< ¼ tsp". Secondary amounts are only within the same kind of unit (tsp↔tbsp↔cup, oz/lb→g).

## Never guessed

Volume↔weight (cups↔grams) is never converted without a recorded ingredient-specific factor. That feature is planned; until then those costs show as incomplete.

## Eggs

Whole eggs are counts. When scaling gives a fraction, the app suggests lightly beating whole eggs and weighing about 50 g per large egg (18 g per yolk for yolk lines). It never suggests separating eggs.

## Brown butter

The baking session records butter weight before and after browning and shows the moisture lost. It never adds cream or water automatically. If the recipe itself lists a "Butter adjustment" group, that is shown; otherwise the app explains that cream is not equivalent to the lost water.
